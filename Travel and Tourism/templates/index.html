Welcome to the Travel and Tourism Management Dashboard

This platform allows you to manage and monitor various aspects of your travel and tourism business. From managing users, destinations, and trips to handling bookings, payments, and reviews, everything you need to run a successful travel management business is at your fingertips.

Explore the different sections in the sidebar to get started with managing your resources effectively and efficiently.

Manage Users

Create and manage users, including staff and customers, with ease. Add new users and view existing ones.

Explore Destinations

Add new destinations and explore a collection of top travel spots for your customers to visit.

Book Your Trips

Manage customer trip bookings, from initial plans to confirmation of travel arrangements.

Discover the world's top tourist destinations.

Customer Reviews

Collect and manage customer feedback on trips, destinations, and guides. Ensure a high level of customer satisfaction.

Tour Guides

Add and manage tour guides who assist travelers during their trips. View their availability and schedule.
